Personal Training for Meditators
Strength

Keeping things easy on purpose. This block is about enjoyment and continuity, not capacity.

Arrive

May I enjoy things just as they are?

Say it once before you begin, then rest in whatever is there. Drop it in mid-rep if it helps, or just move. If another practice feels more alive, use that instead.

The circuit

1
Stork squat
5–10 each side
The support leg is there for balance only — under a tenth of your weight. Keep the pelvis level: "a platter of champagne glasses that never tips"
2
Push-up, from the knees
5–10
Slow down, slow up.
3
Kickstand hinge
5–10 each side
The back leg is the kickstand. A slight bend in the front knee that stays exactly where it is. A mirror or your phone will tell you if it's turning into a squat.

The dose

Slow.30 to 60 seconds between movements. Two rounds if you do it in one go — that's the whole session. Or spread it.One round is one triplet. Do one triplet in the morning, a second in the afternoon. Either works! Two times a week is the minimum,four or five is the maximum.

If a movement starts to feel easy or you're unsure of how to progress, just shoot me a DM.

Running

Fifteen intervals of 1:30 running, 0:30 walking. No frequency goal any more — a range, with both ends respected.

Frequency

One a week is the floor. Enough to hold what's built. Four is the ceiling.
